System Architecture: Sensor-Actuated Model

Replaces vision with all-weather physical sensor arrays and Dilemma Zone Protection (DZP):

  • S1: Inductive Loops (15m): Precise vehicle presence & stopped queue counting.
  • S2: Dilemma Zone Sensors (50m): Prevents red-light running by extending green for fast vehicles.
  • S3: mmWave Doppler Radar (100m): Tracks advance approach speeds & arrival ETAs (t = d / v).
  • VAC V-BOT Decision Engine: Computes weighted urgency f(R,G,R,G) = g(∑ x_i S_i).
